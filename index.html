THE MAN BEHIND THE PLAYS

William Shakespeare

William Shakespeare was an English playwright, poet and actor whose works became some of the most widely performed and studied plays in the world.

Early Life

Shakespeare was born in Stratford-upon-Avon in 1564. He grew up during the Elizabethan period, a time when theatre and literature were becoming increasingly popular.

Life in London

Shakespeare later became closely involved with London's theatre world. He worked as an actor and playwright and became associated with the acting company known as the Lord Chamberlain's Men.

His Famous Works

His plays include tragedies such as Hamlet, Macbeth and Othello, comedies such as Twelfth Night and A Midsummer Night's Dream, and historical plays such as Henry V and Richard III.

His Legacy

Shakespeare's works continue to be performed around the world. His plays explore themes such as love, ambition, jealousy, power, betrayal and human nature.

THE STAGE

The Globe Theatre

The Globe was one of the most famous playhouses of Shakespeare's London. It became closely associated with Shakespeare and his acting company, the Lord Chamberlain's Men.

🏛️

The Theatre

The Globe was a large circular or polygonal open-air playhouse. Its stage projected into the audience, allowing spectators to surround the performance on several sides.

👥

The Audience

People from different social backgrounds attended performances. Some spectators stood close to the stage, while wealthier members could pay for more comfortable seating.

🎭

The Performances

Plays relied heavily on the actors' voices, costumes, movement and the imagination of the audience. Elaborate scenery was limited compared with modern theatre.

☀️

Daylight Theatre

The Globe was an open-air theatre, so performances generally took place during daylight hours rather than at night.

Did You Know?

Shakespeare's Globe was originally built in 1599 on the south bank of the River Thames. The theatre was later rebuilt in 1614 after the original building was destroyed by fire.
