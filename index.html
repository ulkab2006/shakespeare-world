THE STORIES

Explore Shakespeare's Plays

Shakespeare's plays explore ambition, love, betrayal, power, comedy and tragedy. Discover some of his most famous works by genre.

Tragedies

Hamlet

A prince struggles with grief, revenge and the question of what it means to act.

Macbeth

Ambition and prophecy lead Macbeth and Lady Macbeth towards a tragic downfall.

Othello

A tragedy of jealousy, trust and manipulation surrounding the Venetian general Othello.

King Lear

An ageing king divides his kingdom and faces betrayal, suffering and loss.

Comedies

A Midsummer Night's Dream

Lovers, fairies and actors become entangled in a magical forest.

Twelfth Night

Disguise and mistaken identity create a complicated story of love and humour.

Much Ado About Nothing

Wit, romance and misunderstandings collide in one of Shakespeare's popular comedies.

Histories

Henry V

King Henry faces war and leadership during a pivotal period in English history.

Richard III

Richard's pursuit of the English crown drives this dark historical drama.

Richard II

A king's rule is challenged as questions of power and legitimacy emerge.
